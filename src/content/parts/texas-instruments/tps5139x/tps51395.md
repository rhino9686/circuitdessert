## How it works

The TPS51395 is a synchronous buck converter with both power FETs inside the package, 19.5 mΩ on the high side and 9.5 mΩ on the low side, for loads to 8 A from a 4.5 to 24 V input. It uses D-CAP3 control, which needs no compensation network and responds quickly to load steps. It differs from the [TPS51393](site:parts/texas-instruments/tps51393/) only in the output and LDO voltage: 5.1 V and 5.0 V here, 3.36 V and 3.3 V there.

**Output setting.** The output is fixed at 5.1 V. A resistor and capacitor in parallel from VOUT to FB (Rff 240 kΩ and Cff 470 pF in the datasheet's Table 2) feed the output ripple to the FB pin. There is no divider to pick.

**Light load.** The part runs in forced continuous mode at heavy load and changes to Eco-mode (DCM) at light load. Out-of-audio (OOA) operation keeps the switching frequency above audible frequencies toward no load, with a 40 µs period.

**Soft start and protection.** The soft start is internal, 1.25 ms. The converter has a valley current limit (8.1 A minimum), a latched output overvoltage at 123% and undervoltage at 64%, each with a 250 µs deglitch, and thermal shutdown at 150 °C with 20 °C of hysteresis that restarts by itself. Power good has a 1 ms delay.

**LDO.** A 100 mA 5.0 V LDO runs from the feedback line. It is enabled by ENLDO and switches over to the buck output once VOUT reaches 98%, switching back at 96%. A 4.7 µF capacitor is the minimum on the LDO pin.

### Worked example: 5.1 V at 8 A from 19 V

1. **Ratio.** VOUT ÷ VIN = 5.1 ÷ 19 = 0.268, so the high-side FET is on about that fraction of each cycle (calculated).
2. **Ripple.** With the 1.5 µH inductor and 625 kHz, ΔI = 5.1 × (1 − 0.268) ÷ (1.5 µH × 625 kHz) = 3.98 A peak to peak (calculated). The valley current at 8 A load is then about 6.01 A, below the 8.1 A minimum valley limit.
3. **Range.** At 24 V in the ripple rises to 4.28 A; the table above gives 12, 19 and 24 V.

## Layout

The datasheet recommends a four-layer board with a full ground plane and two capacitors right across VIN. Inner layer 1 is ground with the PGND-to-AGND tie, inner layer 2 is a VIN pour with vias to the top, and the bottom layer is ground with the SW routing. Put multiple vias under the device near VIN and PGND, make the VIN trace wide, and keep feedback wide and away from the switch node, since the LDO input comes from the feedback line. The KiCad symbol is ours, built from the pin table, and its Footprint field is empty: no footprint on this site has been compared with this part's package drawing. Pin 1 is BOOT.

## Things to watch

- **Non-public source.** The datasheet we have is a selective-disclosure copy.
- **Latched protection.** OVP and UVP latch the converter off.
- **Output current.** 8 A is the recommended maximum and 10 A the absolute maximum, so ripple counts against it.
- **Tie NC pins to PGND.** Pins 10 and 16.
- **LDO input.** The LDO runs from the feedback line, so FB routing matters.
- **Related part.** The [TPS51393](site:parts/texas-instruments/tps51393/) has the same circuit with a 3.36 V output and a 3.3 V LDO.
- **The KiCad symbol is ours and not yet opened in KiCad.** It was generated by script and checked against the pin table, but nobody has loaded it in KiCad. The pin types and the symbol layout are our choices. The thermal pad is pin 21, as the pin table gives it.

## Datasheet notes

- **Typo.** The abstract says the TPS51395 "provides fixe 5.1V".
- **EN thresholds.** The table gives 1.3–1.5 V rising and 0.8–1.12 V falling; the text gives about 1.2 V and 1.05 V.
- **PGOOD deglitch.** The text says 64 µs for the error deglitch; elsewhere it is 250 µs.
- **Standby.** The text calls the 60 µA standby current "shutdown current".
- **LDO voltage.** The feature list gives a 5 V, 100 mA LDO for both parts; the text says the TPS51393 has a 3.3 V LDO and the TPS51395 a 5.0 V LDO.
- **VCC capacitor.** The text allows 1 µF or 4.7 µF.
- **What was compared.** The Figure 11 circuit was compared with a rendered image; everything else came from the text layer, so the page stays unverified.
