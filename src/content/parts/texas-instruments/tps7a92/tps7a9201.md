## How it works

The TPS7A92 is a low-noise LDO built around a 0.8 V reference that can be filtered with an external capacitor. The error amplifier compares the FB pin with the reference, a charge pump drives the pass device so the input can be as low as 1.4 V, and a PSRR-boost circuit improves rejection between 200 kHz and 1 MHz, where switching regulators put their noise.

**Setting the output.** R1 = R2 × (VOUT ÷ VREF − 1), with R1 from OUT to FB and R2 from FB to ground. The datasheet wants the divider to carry more than 5 µA (VREF ÷ R2) for the best noise, and recommends 0.1%-tolerance resistors if accuracy matters, since the 1% figure doesn't include them. A feed-forward capacitor CFF across R1 improves noise and PSRR; 10 nF is the datasheet's value. A larger CFF slows start-up while the power-good time constant stays the same, which can make PG report a settled output too early; a capacitor in parallel with the pull-up matches the two.

**Noise reduction and soft start.** CNR/SS filters the reference noise and sets the soft-start ramp. tSS = VREF × CNR/SS ÷ INR/SS, with INR/SS 6.2 µA typical when SS_CTRL is grounded and 100 µA typical when SS_CTRL is tied to IN. The datasheet recommends 10 nF to 10 µF, with little noise benefit above 1 µF. If no CNR/SS is used, SS_CTRL must go to ground; tied to IN with no capacitor, the output overshoots by about 10%.

**Power-good.** PG is open drain and goes low when FB falls below 88.9% of the reference, typical, and releases 1% higher. A pull-up of 10 to 100 kΩ is required. PG is valid only above the minimum input, and below about 0.8 V in the open-drain device loses gate drive and PG floats high; pulling PG up to the output reduces that.

**Enable and discharge.** EN is active high, with 0.4 V maximum low and 1.1 V minimum high. Tie EN to IN if unused. When disabled, a 250 Ω switch discharges the output.

**Protection.** The current limit is 2.3 to 2.9 A, folding back to about 2.2 A in a hard short. Thermal shutdown trips at 160 °C and resets at 140 °C. The UVLO reacts quickly to glitches, so the input capacitor should slow input transients to under 2 V/µs.

**Capacitors.** 22 µF or more of ceramic on the output (11 µF effective), or 10 µF (5 µF effective) at 1 A or less. 10 µF or more on each IN pin. The datasheet suggests derating ceramics by 50%.

### Worked example: 1.2 V from 2.0 V at up to 1.5 A

1. **Divider.** 1.2 V ÷ 17.7 kΩ is 68 µA in the divider, above the 5 µA minimum (calculated).
2. **Soft start.** With SS_CTRL at IN and 100 nF, tSS = 0.8 V × 100 nF ÷ 100 µA = 0.8 ms, which matches the 800 µs the datasheet gives. The design requirement was under 2 ms.
3. **Headroom.** 2.0 V less 1.2 V is 0.8 V against a 400 mV dropout maximum.
4. **Heat.** 0.8 V × 1.5 A is 1.2 W. The datasheet says that is a 91 °C rise, 124 °C at 55 °C ambient. The table's RθJA of 62.5 °C/W would give about 75 °C (calculated), so the datasheet figure is the more cautious one.

## Layout

The datasheet's guidelines: put all components on the same side and close to their pins; make the ground returns of the input and output capacitors and the GND pin short, joined by wide component-side copper; avoid vias and long traces on the connections. For the best noise, Figure 7-5 separates the quiet analog ground from the power ground. CNR/SS and R2 go back to the thermal pad on their own connection, and the GND pin goes straight to the thermal pad, not to an external plane. Take the feedback trace to R1 from the load, so the drop to the load is inside the loop. An array of thermal vias under the pad takes heat to the ground planes.

The package is a 2.5 × 2.5 mm 10-pin WSON (DSK). This site has no KiCad symbol or footprint for this part yet.

## Things to watch

- **The dropout figure.** The feature list says 180 mV typical and the description says 180 mV maximum; the table says 400 mV maximum at 2 A, and the overview says 400 mV maximum.
- **SS_CTRL with no CNR/SS.** Ground it, or the output overshoots by about 10%.
- **Large CFF slows start-up.** PG can then report a settled output before it is; add a capacitor across the pull-up.
- **Heat.** At 2 A, each volt of input above the output is 2 W, which is more than the 62.5 °C/W package can lose at 125 °C.
- **Accuracy and the divider.** The 1% excludes the resistors; use 0.1% parts for a tight output.
- **Output capacitance is effective.** 11 µF effective at full load, so size the nominal value for DC-bias derating.
- **Input transients.** The UVLO trips on a fast-collapsing input; slow it below 2 V/µs.
- **The output is discharged when disabled.** 250 Ω typical.
