## How it works

The TPS51200 is a linear regulator that can both source and sink current, which a DDR termination rail needs because the memory bus pulls VTT in both directions. VO follows the voltage on REFIN. For DDR memory REFIN is VDDQ ÷ 2, made with two equal resistors; any other application can put its own reference on REFIN anywhere between 0.5 and 1.8 V.

**Supplies.** VIN (2.375 to 3.5 V) powers the control circuits, so it comes from a 2.5 V or 3.3 V rail. VLDOIN (1.1 to 3.5 V) feeds the output stage, and the datasheet's example connects it to VDDQ, which keeps the dissipation low. The output current comes through VLDOIN when sourcing and goes to PGND when sinking.

**Remote sensing.** VOSNS goes to the output capacitors at the load. The datasheet adds that if their ESR is above 2 mΩ, an RC filter in the sense path helps stability. In the design example VOSNS is tied to VO next to the capacitors.

**Reference output.** REFOUT is a buffered copy of REFIN that can supply ±10 mA, for the memory's VREF input. Its capacitor must be 0.1 µF, and the total on the pin must stay below 0.47 µF.

**Enable and tracking.** For a DDR rail EN goes to the SLP_S3 signal, so VTT is off in suspend-to-RAM while VDDQ stays on; for other uses EN is a plain on/off input. If EN is tied to the system bus instead, VO tracks REFOUT as REFIN rises and falls, and PGOOD is asserted 2 ms after VO is within ±20% of REFOUT. The output is discharged through 18 Ω when the part is off.

**Power-good.** PGOOD is open drain and releases 2 ms after VO is inside ±20% of REFOUT; it goes low after a 10 µs deglitch time when VO leaves the window.

**Protection.** There are current limits of 3 to 4.5 A (source) and 3.5 to 5.5 A (sink), a VIN undervoltage lockout near 2.3 V, a REFIN lockout at 390 mV and thermal shutdown at 150 °C with 25 °C of hysteresis.

**Capacitors.** At least 20 µF of ceramic on VO (the example uses three 10 µF), 4.7 µF on VIN and two 10 µF on VLDOIN. The datasheet's Bode plot for the example shows about 1 MHz of bandwidth and 52° of phase margin.

## Example application

Figure 7-1 of the datasheet is a 3.3 V input, DDR3 design: VDDQ and VLDOIN are 1.5 V, so VTT is 0.75 V. REFIN comes from two 10 kΩ resistors with a 1000 pF capacitor, VO has three 10 µF capacitors, VOSNS is tied to VO, VLDOIN has two 10 µF capacitors, VIN has 4.7 µF, PGOOD is pulled up to VIN through 100 kΩ, REFOUT has 0.1 µF and EN is driven by SLP_S3.

### Worked example: DDR3 termination at ±2 A

1. **Voltage.** Two equal resistors give REFIN = 1.5 V ÷ 2 = 0.75 V, inside the 0.5–1.8 V range, and VTT follows it to within ±25 mV at ±2 A (calculated).
2. **Heat at a steady 2 A.** The datasheet's Equations 5 and 6 give the dissipation: sourcing 2 A from VLDOIN = 1.5 V to VO = 0.75 V is (1.5 − 0.75) × 2 = 1.5 W, and sinking 2 A is 0.75 × 2 = 1.5 W. With RθJA of 84.6 °C/W that is a 127 °C rise (calculated), which passes the 125 °C junction limit at any ambient above about −2 °C. So ±2 A cannot be a continuous load in this circuit; our reading is that it is a peak, since the average termination current of a memory bus is much smaller.
3. **Supply current.** 3.3 V × 0.7 mA is about 2 mW of quiescent loss on VIN.

## Layout

The datasheet's guidelines: put the input capacitors close to the VLDOIN pin and the output capacitor close to VO, both with short, wide connections; if the rest of the output capacitance sits at the load, keep at least a 10 µF ceramic at the VO pin. Take VOSNS from the positive node of the output capacitors as a separate trace, to the DIMM side in a DDR application so VTT is regulated where it is used, and add a low-pass filter if that trace is very long. Connect GND and PGND directly to the thermal pad, and put numerous ground vias in the pad under a large surface copper plane, since the pad is what removes the heat. The datasheet points to the TPS51200EVM user's guide (SLUU323) for more.

The package is a 3 × 3 mm 10-pin VSON (DRC) with a thermal pad. This site has no KiCad symbol or footprint for this part yet. Pin 1 is REFIN.

## Things to watch

- **Heat limits the real current.** At 2 A the dissipation is 1.5 W in the example, and the package's 84.6 °C/W cannot lose that at 85 °C ambient.
- **VLDOIN sets the loss.** The datasheet says a VLDOIN supply lower than VDDQ cuts the power lost when sourcing (its example ties VLDOIN to VDDQ).
- **REFOUT capacitor.** It must be 0.1 µF, with less than 0.47 µF in total.
- **Output capacitor ESR and the sense trace.** The datasheet suggests an RC filter in the VOSNS path when ESR is above 2 mΩ, and a low-pass filter when the sense trace is very long.
- **Enable and tracking.** EN should be driven by SLP_S3 for DDR; tied to the bus, VTT tracks the reference instead.
- **Category.** This site has no category for termination regulators; the page is filed under "Other".
- **Operating temperature.** The ambient range is −40 to 85 °C.
