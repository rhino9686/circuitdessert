## How it works

The SY6103 is a 3 A low-dropout regulator with a PMOS pass element. A 1.24 V reference and an error amplifier compare the feedback pin to the reference, and the output is set by two resistors: VOUT = 1.24 V × (1 + R1 ÷ R2). The datasheet allows 1 kΩ to 1 MΩ for the resistors.

**Enable.** EN is active high (2.4 V minimum on, 0.8 V maximum off). In shutdown the supply current is about 1 µA.

**Soft start.** The output rises over about 2 ms (4 ms maximum).

**Short-circuit protection.** If the output falls below about 50% of its set voltage (40 to 60%), the regulator turns off and restarts after about 38 ms, repeating while the short remains. Over-temperature shutdown is at 150 °C typical with 20 °C of hysteresis.

**Capacitors.** About 10 µF X5R (25 V) on the input and at least 2.2 µF on the output; the datasheet says 2.2 µF works. The datasheet also suggests a small ceramic capacitor in parallel with R1 to speed up the load-transient response in applications with large load steps.

### Worked example: 5 V in, 3.3 V out at 3 A

1. **Divider.** With R2 = 10 kΩ, R1 = 16.5 kΩ gives 1.24 × (1 + 1.65) = 3.286 V (calculated).
2. **Headroom.** Dropout at 3 A is 700 mV maximum, so 5 V has plenty of room above 3.3 V.
3. **Heat.** (5 − 3.3) × 3 = 5.1 W (calculated). With θJA 24.5 °C/W that is a 125 °C rise, so 3 A is not a continuous load on the datasheet's board; at 85 °C ambient the limit is about 0.96 A. See the calculated table.

## Layout

The datasheet says the input and output capacitors must connect directly to the IN, OUT and GND pins with traces that carry no other current, and that CIN and COUT should sit close to the device with short traces. The feedback loop formed by R1, R2 and the trace from FB to OUT should be kept small, the regulator ground pin should go to circuit ground at a single point, and a large copper area helps thermal performance. It includes a PCB layout drawing. The KiCad symbol and the TO263-5 and TO252-5 footprint are ours, built from the pin table and the package drawing (see the package page). Pin 1 is EN.

## Things to watch

- **Power, not current, sets the limit.** On the datasheet's board the part cannot carry 3 A when the input-to-output drop is large.
- **Input overvoltage.** Every pin is rated 19 V absolute maximum, so an 18 V rail leaves only 1 V of margin.
- **Hiccup restart.** A short produces repeated restarts rather than a steady current limit.
- **Minimum output capacitance.** Keep at least 2.2 µF on OUT.
- **The KiCad files are ours and not yet opened in KiCad.** The symbol and the TO263-5 and TO252-5 footprint were generated by script and checked against the pin table and the datasheet's drawing, but nobody has loaded them in KiCad. The pin types and the symbol layout are our choices.
