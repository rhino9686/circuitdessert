## How it works

The SQ24345H is a high-input-voltage adjustable linear regulator with a 500 mA rating. A 1.235 V reference and error amplifier set the output through a divider: VOUT = 1.235 V × (1 + R1 ÷ R2), with 10 kΩ to 10 MΩ allowed for the resistors. A capacitor across R1 is optional.

**Enable and discharge.** EN is active high (1.5 V on, 0.4 V off). When the part is off, a 600 Ω resistor discharges the output.

**Protection.** The current limit is 900 mA typical (600 to 1200 mA), and over-temperature shutdown is at 150 °C with 20 °C of hysteresis.

**Heat.** The package's rated 2.17 W at 25 °C limits how much input-to-output drop can be taken at load; see the calculated table.

**Capacitors.** About 10 µF on IN and 4.7 µF on OUT.

### Worked example: 12 V in, 3.3 V at 200 mA

1. **Divider.** 1.235 × (1 + 50.1 ÷ 30) = 3.297 V (calculated).
2. **Heat.** (12 − 3.3) × 0.2 = 1.74 W (calculated). With θJA 46 °C/W the junction rises about 80 °C, so it reaches 125 °C at about 45 °C ambient. At 250 mA the 25 °C limit is already reached.
3. **Headroom.** The dropout at 500 mA is 750 mV maximum, which is no problem from 12 V.

## Layout

The datasheet says to keep all power traces short and wide, to use a 2-layer or 4-layer board for thermal performance, and to place the input and output capacitors close to the IC. ## Things to watch

- **Power sets the limit.** At 24 V in and 5 V out the part can carry only about 115 mA at 25 °C.
- **Ground current at full load.** It reaches 6.5 mA typical and 30 mA maximum at 500 mA, much more than the 7 µA at no load.
- **Absolute maximum 40 V,** only 4 V above the top of the operating range.
- **NC pins.** Pins 2, 4 and 6 are not connected.
- **Initial release.** Rev. 0.9 values may change.
- **The KiCad files are ours and not yet opened in KiCad.** The symbol and the DFN2×3-8 footprint were generated by script and checked against the pin table and the datasheet's drawing, but nobody has loaded them in KiCad.
