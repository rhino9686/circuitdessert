## Design notes

Its adjustable output (0.6 V reference) makes it easy to pick a rail with two resistors. The linux-sunxi wiki notes that some NanoPi and Orange Pi boards use it for their core supply, switching between 1.1 V and 1.3 V with a GPIO-driven resistor array, since the chip has no I²C or digital voltage control.

The older SY8113 (non-B) is limited to 16 V input. Check the suffix before reusing a design.

The specifications above come from AN_SY8113B Rev 0.1, an application note marked "Silergy Corp. Confidential, prepared for Internal Use Only" that we found on a third-party distributor's site, not Silergy's. Treat them as unconfirmed until they are checked against a current Silergy datasheet. The [SY8113B recipe](site:recipes/sy8113b/) works through the note's circuit and lists the points where the note is silent.
