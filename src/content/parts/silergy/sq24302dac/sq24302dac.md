## How it works

The SQ24302DAC is a 2 A low-dropout regulator with a 0.5 V reference. The output is set by two resistors: VOUT = 0.5 V × (R3 + R2) ÷ R2, or R2 = 0.5 V ÷ (VOUT − 0.5 V) × R3. The datasheet recommends 10 kΩ to 1 MΩ for both and gives R3 = 56 kΩ, R2 = 10 kΩ for 3.3 V.

**Enable.** EN is active high (1.2 V on, 0.4 V off). The pin table says the part is enabled if EN is left open; the application text says to tie EN to the input if the function is not used.

**Reverse blocking.** The output cannot push current back into the input when the output is held above the input.

**Protection.** The current limit is 3 A typical (2.1 to 4.4 A). Over-temperature shutdown is at 150 °C with 20 °C of hysteresis.

**Capacitors.** At least 10 µF on IN and a minimum of 22 µF on OUT. A minimum load of 10 µA is specified.

### Worked example: 1.8 V in, 1.2 V at 2 A

1. **Divider.** With R3 = 56 kΩ and R2 = 40.2 kΩ, 0.5 × (96.2 ÷ 40.2) = 1.1965 V (calculated).
2. **Headroom.** The 0.6 V drop is above the 400 mV maximum dropout at 2 A for VIN of 1.6 V and above, so the input is high enough.
3. **Heat.** (1.8 − 1.2) × 2 = 1.2 W (calculated); with θJA 41 °C/W the junction rises about 49 °C.

## Layout

The datasheet says to keep the VIN, OUT and GND power traces short and wide and to use at least 2-ounce copper for them, to put a ground plane under all circuitry, and to place the input and output capacitors close to the part and connected to the ground plane. ## Things to watch

- **22 µF minimum on the output.** This is more than most LDOs of this size need.
- **Dropout is higher near the bottom of the input range.** For VIN of 1.5 to 1.6 V it is 600 mV maximum at 2 A.
- **Minimum load of 10 µA.**
- **Pins 1, 6 and 8 are NC.**
- **Absolute maximum 7 V** on every pin, 1 V above the top of the operating range.
- **The KiCad files are ours and not yet opened in KiCad.** The symbol and the DFN3×3-8 footprint were generated by script and checked against the pin table and the datasheet's drawing, but nobody has loaded them in KiCad.
