## How it works

The SY6355 is a DDR termination regulator that both sources and sinks current. The termination voltage VTT (the VO pin) tracks the REFIN input, which in the datasheet's circuit is made from the memory supply VDDQ through a 10 kΩ and 10 kΩ divider. VIN powers the control circuit and VLDOIN the output stage, which allows the output stage to run from the memory rail (for example 1.5 V for DDR3).

**Reference output.** REFOUT is a buffered copy of REFIN (±12 mV) that can drive the memory's reference input. It turns on when REFIN reaches 0.390 V (with VIN above its UVLO), can source or sink 10 mA, stays on when EN is low and discharges through an internal 10 kΩ below 0.375 V.

**Remote sense.** VOSNS is the output voltage sense pin. It connects to the positive node of the output capacitors as a separate trace, and on the DIMM side in a DDR VTT design. If the output capacitor ESR is above 2 mΩ, add an RC between the output and VOSNS.

**Power good.** PGOOD is open drain and goes high when VO is within ±20% of REFOUT, with a 2 ms delay on the way up and a 10 µs delay on the way down. It needs a 1 kΩ to 100 kΩ pull-up.

**Protection.** The output current limit is cut in half while the output is outside the power-good window. There are UVLOs on VIN (2.375 V) and REFIN (0.39 V) and an over-temperature shutdown at 150 °C with 20 °C of hysteresis. EN must not be taken above VIN.

**Capacitors.** CVIN 1 to 4.7 µF; VLDOIN at least 10 µF (about half of the output capacitance); VO at least 20 µF in total (3 × 10 µF).

### Worked example: DDR3 at 2 A

1. **Rails.** VDDQ and VLDOIN at 1.5 V, and the equal 10 kΩ divider puts REFIN at 0.75 V (calculated, ignoring the 1 µA REFIN current), so VTT is 0.75 V.
2. **Heat.** Sourcing dissipates (1.5 − 0.75) × 2 = 1.5 W and sinking dissipates 0.75 × 2 = 1.5 W (calculated).
3. **Limit.** With θJA 54.7 °C/W the junction rises about 82 °C, so the part reaches 125 °C at about 43 °C ambient. At 85 °C ambient the limit is about 0.97 A. See the calculated table.

## Layout

The datasheet gives six points: put the VLDOIN capacitors close to the pin and the VO capacitors close to VO, both with short, wide connections (with at least 10 µF next to VO if the rest of the output capacitance is on the load side); run VOSNS as a separate trace from the positive node of the output capacitors, to the DIMM side in a DDR design, with a low-pass filter if it is long; connect GND and PGND directly to the thermal pad; and put numerous ground vias on the thermal pad under a large ground copper area. It includes a PCB layout drawing. The KiCad symbol and the DFN3×3-10 footprint are ours, built from the pin table and the package drawing (see the package page). Pin 1 is REFIN.

## Things to watch

- **VIN is limited to 3.5 V.** It suits the 2.5 V and 3.3 V rails, not 5 V.
- **EN must not exceed VIN.**
- **Output capacitance.** At least 20 µF on VO; with higher ESR, add an RC to VOSNS.
- **REFOUT stays on** when EN is low.
- **Current limit halves** while the output is out of the power-good window.
- **Power, not current,** sets the limit at high ambient temperatures.
- **The KiCad files are ours and not yet opened in KiCad.** The symbol and the DFN3×3-10 footprint were generated by script and checked against the pin table and the datasheet's drawing, but nobody has loaded them in KiCad. The pin types and the symbol layout are our choices. The exposed pad's number is ours.

- **Document type.** The file is titled an "Application Note", Rev. 0.9, and has the same electrical tables as a datasheet.
