## How it works

The SY6307B splits the supplies. IN feeds the PMOS pass element and can go as low as the output plus the dropout (0.8 V minimum), while a separate Bias input feeds the control circuit and needs 2.4 to 5.5 V. The datasheet requires Bias to be about 1.4 V above the output, so the output tops out at Bias − 1.4 V.

**Output voltage.** VOUT = 0.8 V × (1 + RH ÷ RL), from a 0.8 V reference (±1%).

**Enable.** EN is active high (1.0 V on, 0.4 V off). When disabled, a 130 Ω discharge on the output pulls it down.

**Protection.** There is an output current limit (500 mA minimum), short-circuit protection and over-temperature shutdown at 150 °C with 20 °C of hysteresis.

**Capacitors.** 1 µF on IN, 2.2 µF on OUT (ESR 10 to 50 mΩ) and at least 0.1 µF on Bias.

### Worked example: 1.2 V in, 1.0 V at 500 mA

1. **Output.** 0.8 × (1 + 49.9 ÷ 200) = 0.9996 V (calculated).
2. **Bias.** It must be at least 1.4 V above 1.0 V, so 2.4 V or more; a 3.3 V rail works.
3. **Heat.** The drop is 0.2 V, so PD = 0.2 × 0.5 = 0.1 W (calculated) plus the bias current (110 µA × 3.3 V, about 0.4 mW), and with θJA 70 °C/W the rise is about 7 °C. The 0.2 V drop is above the 90 mV typical dropout.

## Layout

The datasheet says the input and output capacitors must connect directly to the IN, OUT and GND pins with traces carrying no other current, with CIN and COUT near the device on short traces, and that large copper area helps thermal performance. It includes a recommended PCB layout drawing. The KiCad symbol and the DFN1.2×1.2-6 footprint are ours, built from the pin table and the package drawing (see the package page). Pin 1 is OUT.

## Things to watch

- **Two supplies.** The bias rail must be present and high enough, or the output cannot reach its set voltage.
- **Bias headroom.** Bias must exceed the output by 1.4 V, so a 3.3 V bias limits the output to 1.9 V and a 2.5 V bias to 1.1 V.
- **Small package.** 1.4 W is the package rating; at 85 °C the usable power is far lower.
- **Absolute maximum 6.0 V** on every pin.
- **The KiCad files are ours and not yet opened in KiCad.** The symbol and the DFN1.2×1.2-6 footprint were generated by script and checked against the pin table and the datasheet's drawing, but nobody has loaded them in KiCad. The pin types and the symbol layout are our choices. The package's exposed pad is not in the pin table, so the symbol has no pin for it.
