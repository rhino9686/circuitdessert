## How it works

The SA21345G is a high-input-voltage linear regulator with a fixed 5 V output and a 150 mA load rating. It has a PMOS pass element, an under-voltage lockout, a 1 ms soft start, an output current limit and a short-circuit shutdown that retries.

**Short-circuit hiccup.** If VOUT falls below about 0.8 V (0.4 to 1.5 V over the part), the regulator shuts down for about 16 ms and restarts with a full soft-start cycle. If the short is still there it repeats, until the over-temperature protection (150 °C, with 20 °C of hysteresis) trips instead.

**Capacitors.** Put a ceramic X5R or better capacitor close to IN and GND (10 µF recommended) and a 4.7 µF low-ESR ceramic on OUT. The datasheet says larger output capacitors such as 22 µF reduce noise and improve transient response and PSRR.

**Heat.** Dropout is small, but the drop from a 12 V or 24 V supply to 5 V is not: see the calculated table above. The datasheet's own rating of 0.926 W at 25 °C ambient is the limit.

### Worked example: 12 V in, 5 V at 150 mA

1. **Dissipation.** (12 − 5) × 0.15 = 1.05 W, plus the 15 µA quiescent current times 12 V, which is under 0.2 mW (calculated).
2. **Against the rating.** That is above the 0.926 W the datasheet gives at 25 °C ambient. With θJA 108 °C/W the junction rises about 113 °C, so the junction reaches 125 °C at an ambient of about 12 °C and the part would reach its 150 °C thermal shutdown a little above 36 °C ambient (calculated).
3. **What works.** At 12 V in and 25 °C ambient, the calculated table gives about 132 mA, and at 24 V in about 49 mA. The full 150 mA is only practical at inputs up to about 11 V at 25 °C.

## Layout

The datasheet says to keep all power traces short and wide, to use a 2-layer or 4-layer board for heat, and to put the input and output capacitors close to the IC. It gives a layout drawing with CIN beside IN and COUT beside OUT. This site has no KiCad symbol or footprint for this part yet. Pin 1 is OUT.

## Things to watch

- **Power, not current, sets the limit.** At 24 V in the part can supply only about 49 mA at 25 °C on the datasheet's board.
- **Operating range versus the title.** The title says 40 V; the operating range is 4 to 36 V and 40 V is the absolute maximum.
- **Hiccup restart.** A short produces repeating 16 ms shutdowns with soft-start restarts, not a steady current limit.
- **Four NC pins.** They are not connected inside, so they can be left open.
- **Preliminary document.** Rev. 0.9 is an initial release and the electrical characteristics are guaranteed by design or statistical correlation.
