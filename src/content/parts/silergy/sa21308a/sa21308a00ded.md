## How it works

The SA21308A is a 500 mA low-dropout regulator with a PMOS pass element, a 0.55 V reference and an error amplifier, for rails from 1.62 to 6 V. The output is set with two resistors: VOUT = 0.55 V × (1 + RH ÷ RL), or RL = 0.55 V ÷ (VOUT − 0.55 V) × RH. The datasheet recommends 1 kΩ to 1 MΩ for both and gives 1.8 V with a 100 kΩ RH as a 44 kΩ RL.

**Enable.** EN is active high (above 1.1 V on, below 0.3 V off) and must be tied to IN if the function is not used. When disabled, an internal pull-down of about 95 Ω discharges the output.

**Power good.** PG is open drain and goes high-impedance when VFB rises above 92% of its target (88–96%), and is pulled low when it falls below 90% (85–94%), or on OTP or UVLO. It needs a 10 kΩ to 100 kΩ pull-up to a stable rail and can be left floating or tied to ground if not used.

**Protection.** The current limit regulates at 790 mA typical. If VOUT falls below about 40% of its set point, the part folds back to a short-circuit limit of about 300 mA to cut dissipation. Over-temperature shutdown is at 170 °C with a 155 °C reset, and it recovers automatically.

**Feedforward capacitor.** The compensation is internal. A 10 pF to 1 nF ceramic in parallel with RH can improve load-transient response; the datasheet's Figure 1 shows an optional 100 pF.

**Capacitors.** A 1 µF X7R (or better) input capacitor and at least 2.2 µF on the output, both close to the pins.

## Example application

Figure 1 of the datasheet is a 3.3 V output (RH 100 kΩ, RL 20 kΩ) with a 1 µF input capacitor, a 2.2 µF output capacitor, an optional 100 pF feedforward capacitor across RH, a 100 kΩ pull-up on PG and a 1 MΩ resistor with a jumper on EN. ### Worked example: 3.3 V at 500 mA from 5 V

1. **Output.** 0.55 V × (1 + 100 kΩ ÷ 20 kΩ) = 3.30 V.
2. **Headroom.** The dropout at 500 mA for a 3.3 V output is 185 mV maximum, so the input must be at least about 3.49 V; 5 V is well clear.
3. **Heat.** PD = (5 − 3.3) × 0.5 + 5 × 55 µA = 0.85 W (calculated), which with θJA 68 °C/W is about 58 °C above ambient. The datasheet's limit at 25 °C ambient is 1.83 W.

## Layout

Place the input and output capacitors as close to the device as possible, keep power traces short and wide, and connect the exposed pad to a large ground copper area with several GND vias. The datasheet recommends a 2-layer or 4-layer board. This site has no KiCad symbol or footprint for this part yet. Pin 1 is OUT.

## Things to watch

- **The EN pin can't float.** Tie it to IN if unused.
- **Minimum load.** The recommended output current range starts at 1 mA, not zero.
- **Accuracy excludes the resistors.** Use 1% or better resistors and add their tolerance.
- **Dropout depends on the output voltage.** It is 340 mV maximum for a 1.5 to 2 V output and 185 mV for 3.3 V and above.
- **PG is also low in UVLO and OTP,** not only when the output is out of window.
- **The requested code.** SA21308A12DED is not covered by this datasheet.
