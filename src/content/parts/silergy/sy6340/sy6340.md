## How it works

The SY6340 is a linear regulator with a PMOS pass device, an adjustable output set by an external divider, and a 150 mA load rating. The block diagram shows an under-voltage lockout, a current sense, a thermal detector, an output discharge switch, a soft-start block ("SST") and a 0.6 V reference. The ADJ pin is the feedback input: VOUT = 0.6 V × (1 + R1 ÷ R2), with R1 from OUT to ADJ and R2 from ADJ to ground.

**Choosing the resistors.** The datasheet recommends 10 kΩ to 1 MΩ for both, large values to keep the light-load current down. Its example is 3.3 V with R1 = 100 kΩ, which needs R2 = 22.1 kΩ; the exact value is 22.22 kΩ, so 22.1 kΩ (the nearest standard value) sets 3.315 V (calculated).

**Capacitors.** About 2.2 µF, X5R or better, on the input, close to the pins, and 2.2 µF on the output, for which the part is designed to be stable with very small ceramics. Larger values improve the transient response. The output capacitor's ESR matters: it forms a zero that gives the phase lead the loop needs, but the datasheet gives no ESR range. For large load steps the datasheet recommends a small ceramic capacitor in parallel with R1 (Figure 1 shows 47 pF).

**Protection.** The output current is limited at 350 mA typical (500 mA maximum). The input tolerates −20 V, and the datasheet gives a reverse leakage figure at −15 V. The part shuts down thermally at 150 °C with 20 °C of hysteresis, and in shutdown a 500 Ω resistor discharges the output.

**Enable.** Pull EN low to shut down and high to enable; the datasheet says not to leave it open.

### Worked example: 3.3 V from 5 V at 150 mA

1. **Divider.** R1 = 100 kΩ and R2 = 22.1 kΩ give 3.315 V, 0.45% above 3.3 V. The divider draws 27 µA from the output, more than the 18 µA quiescent current at light load (calculated).
2. **Headroom.** 5 V less 3.3 V is 1.7 V against a dropout of 0.3 V typical at 150 mA. The datasheet gives no maximum dropout.
3. **Dissipation.** (5 − 3.3) × 0.15 A is 0.255 W. The ground current at 150 mA adds 5 V × 450 µA = 2.25 mW, so about 0.257 W (calculated).
4. **Junction temperature.** That is a rise of 26 °C in the SOT23-5 (100 °C/W) and 16 °C in the DFN2×2-6 (62 °C/W), so at 85 °C ambient the junction is at about 111 °C or 101 °C, under the 125 °C limit (calculated).
5. **A higher input.** At 12 V in the same load dissipates 1.30 W, above the 1 W the datasheet gives for the SOT23-5 at 25 °C ambient. At 25 °C the SOT23-5 could carry about 115 mA and the DFN2×2-6 the full 150 mA (calculated, ignoring ground current). See the design table for the highest input at 150 mA.

## Layout

The datasheet says good layout matters: the input and output capacitors must connect directly to the IN, OUT and GND pins with traces that carry no other current, placed close to the device with short traces, and the regulator ground goes to the external circuit ground at a single point. A large copper area helps the thermal performance. Page 9 has a recommended layout drawing for each package, and pages 10 and 11 give the package outlines with a recommended pad layout. Pin 1 of the SOT23-5 is IN; pin 1 of the DFN2×2-6 is EN.

The KiCad symbol file holds one symbol for each package, `SY6340_SOT23-5` and `SY6340_DFN2x2-6`, because the two number the pins differently and only the DFN has the NC pin. No footprint is linked from either symbol: both packages have a package page, but this datasheet's drawings have not been compared with them. The SOT-23-5 page is built from a Diodes drawing and the DFN2×2-6 page from another Silergy datasheet, and this datasheet's drawings have not been compared with them: the SY6340's SOT23-5 recommended pads differ from the ones on the SOT-23-5 page, and its DFN2×2-6 body (1.90 to 2.10 mm) and 0.65 mm pitch match the DFN2×2-6 page but its pads were not checked.

## Things to watch

- **Do not run a short at a high input.** The text says that where an over-current or short circuit is possible the input voltage is not recommended to exceed 9 V, though the operating range is 2.3 to 30 V. At 24 V in, the 350 mA typical limit means about 8.4 W in the package (calculated), against 1 W at 25 °C. The hard-short plots are at 5 V in.
- **Power, not current, limits the part at higher inputs.** The design table shows the highest input at 150 mA; above it the load must come down or the junction passes 125 °C. The thermal resistances are for a two-layer Silergy evaluation board.
- **The enable thresholds cover only 2.8 to 5.5 V.** High is 1.5 V minimum and low 0.6 V maximum, both specified at VIN from 2.8 to 5.5 V, while the input runs to 30 V and EN may go to VIN + 0.3 V. No enable current is given. Do not leave EN open.
- **The reference has no tolerance row.** 0.6 V appears only in the equation and block diagram. The ±2% accuracy is a whole-output figure at 100 µA load, and the datasheet does not say whether it includes the divider. With 1% resistors the divider alone can move the set value by about -1.6% to +1.7% for 100 kΩ and 22.1 kΩ (calculated, worst case). There is no ADJ bias current.
- **No output ESR range.** The text calls the ESR "critical" for stability and gives no limits.
- **Dropout has no maximum.** The four typical points (20, 100, 200 and 300 mV at 10, 50, 100 and 150 mA) all imply 2 Ω (calculated). The lowest input for a 3.3 V output at 150 mA is therefore about 3.6 V typical, our reading and not a datasheet figure.
- **The undervoltage threshold has only a maximum.** The rising threshold is 2.25 V maximum with 100 mV of hysteresis, while the operating range starts at 2.3 V.
- **The output ratings print without a sign.** The absolute maximum and recommended output voltage both read "0.3V+VIN", which looks like −0.3 V to VIN + 0.3 V with the sign lost; the EN line has the same form with its minus sign.
- **DFN2×2-6 details are incomplete.** Pin 2 is NC in the pinout but not in the pin table, and the exposed pad is labelled GND in the pinout but is not numbered or described. The page 9 caption calls the package "DNF2×2-6".
- **Two packages, one datasheet, one status.** Only the SY6340AAC (SOT23-5) product page loaded. Silergy's site also lists a SY6340BAAC (a fixed 3.3 V, 50 mA part) and a SY6340ZDED; this datasheet does not cover them and they have no page here.
- **Initial release.** Rev. 1.0 is the initial production release.
- **The KiCad symbol is ours and not yet opened in KiCad.** It was generated by script from the pin table and checked against it, but nobody has loaded it in KiCad. The DFN2×2-6 symbol has no pin for the exposed pad, which the pinout labels "GND" and the pin table does not list, so the symbol leaves the footprint's pad unconnected. Decide its connection in your layout; the pinout drawing suggests ground. The pin types and the symbol layout are our choices.

## Datasheet notes

- **Test conditions.** The electrical table is headed "VIN = VOUT + 1 V, or VIN = 2.3 V, VEN = VIN, TA = 25 °C", which does not say which applies when VOUT is above 1.3 V.
- **Capacitor test condition.** PSRR is specified with COUT = 10 µF, while the application text and Figure 1 use 2.2 µF.
- **Power ratings.** The 1 W and 1.6 W dissipation figures are consistent with (125 °C − 25 °C) ÷ θJA for 100 °C/W and 62 °C/W (our check).
- **What was compared.** The electrical table was compared with page 3 as rendered, and the numbers match; the performance plots, the layout drawings and the tape-and-reel drawings were not read.
