## How it works

The SC1565 is a low-dropout regulator whose pass drop is small, 270 mV typical at 1.5 A, so the input needs little more than the output plus that drop. It has a 1.200 V reference and an ADJ pin. On the 5-lead and SO-8 parts, the ADJ pin decides the mode: when it is grounded the part uses its internal divider for the preset 1.8 V or 2.5 V option, and when it sits above its threshold (0.05 to 0.40 V) the part regulates ADJ to 1.200 V and the external divider sets the output.

**Package choice.** The 3-lead SOT-223, TO-220 and TO-263 versions have no enable or ADJ pin and come only as 1.8 V or 2.5 V fixed. The SO-8, TO-220-5 and TO-263-5 have both, and their preset voltage can be overridden with resistors.

**Enable.** EN high is 1.6 V and low is 0.4 V. Leaving it open enables the part. In shutdown the supply current is 10 µA typical.

**Capacitors.** It is built for ceramic capacitors: 4.7 µF at the input and 2.2 µF bulk plus 0.1 µF at the output, and the datasheet says it tolerates ESR. The divider's R2 should be 120 kΩ or less so that at least 10 µA flows, and the minimum load of 1 µA typical (10 µA maximum) is then met by the divider.

### Worked example: 2.5 V from a 3.3 V rail

1. **Option.** The preset 2.5 V part needs no resistors: ground ADJ. For another output use the adjustable divider from the first design table.
2. **Headroom.** The input is 3.3 V, so the drop allowed is 0.8 V. At 1.5 A the maximum dropout is 500 mV (600 mV over temperature), so there is margin.
3. **Heat.** The drop is 0.8 V at 1.5 A, 1.2 W. See the second design table: 60 to 78 °C of rise by package.

## Things to watch

- **Only some packages have enable and ADJ.** The 3-lead parts are fixed and always on.
- **Maximum output for 1.8 V parts.** Note (1) on page 1 says 5.4 V, against 4.8 V in the feature list.
- **Input must be 2.2 V or more.** The preset options need VO + 0.7 V in for their specification.
- **Absolute maximum is 7 V.** A 5 V rail with a spike will come close.
- **Keep the divider light but not too light.** R2 over 120 kΩ falls under 10 µA.
- **Status is not established.** The datasheet is dated 2009.
