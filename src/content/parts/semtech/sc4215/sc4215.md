## How it works

The SC4215 is a high-current LDO built for low supply voltages: the input can be as low as 1.6 V, and the dropout is 300 mV typical at 2 A. The ADJ pin selects the mode. A voltage above the 0.16 V typical threshold (0.05 to 0.40 V) makes a "sense select" switch disconnect the internal feedback chain and use the ADJ pin instead; at or near ground the internal divider sets 2.5 V; tied to VO the pin makes the output 0.8 V.

**Resistors.** The datasheet recommends 1% resistors with R2 between 1 kΩ and 50 kΩ, a divider current of at least 10 µA, and gives R1 = R2 × (VO − VREF) ÷ VREF. In adjustable mode the divider also supplies the minimum load of 1 µA typical (10 µA maximum).

**Enable.** Pulling EN below 0.4 V shuts the regulator off; left open it is enabled, and it can be tied to VIN. For supplies below 1.9 V the datasheet says to connect a pull-up of up to 400 kΩ from EN to VIN; above 1.9 V EN may float or tie to VIN.

**Capacitors.** The input wants at least 10 µF per ampere of load as bulk capacitance close to VIN, so the supply does not sag below its minimum, plus a 4.7 µF ceramic right at the pin. The output wants at least 10 µF per ampere of bulk and a 0.1 µF ceramic. The part is designed for ceramics and tolerates ESR, so tantalum works. Several smaller ceramics in parallel cause no stability problem.

**Thermal.** PD is about IO × (VIN − VO); the thermal resistance in the datasheet is for a large four-layer board with thermal vias under the exposed pad.

### Worked example: 1.8 V at 2 A from a 2.5 V rail

1. **Divider.** With R2 = 10 kΩ, R1 = 12.5 kΩ (from the design table). A real 12.4 kΩ or 12.7 kΩ 1% value gives about 1.79 V or 1.82 V (calculated).
2. **Headroom.** 2.5 V less 1.8 V is 0.7 V, above the 0.5 V of the dropout test and the 0.6 V of its other row.
3. **Enable.** The input is above 1.9 V, so EN may be tied to VIN.
4. **Heat.** 0.7 V × 2 A is 1.4 W, so with the datasheet's 36 °C/W a rise of about 50 °C (calculated, on the datasheet's large board).

## Things to watch

- **Input sag.** The part needs 1.6 V at the pin under load, so a weak 1.8 V rail with a big load step can drop out of range.
- **EN below 1.9 V.** Use the pull-up the datasheet describes, up to 400 kΩ to VIN.
- **Adjustable mode needs a load.** The divider should carry at least 10 µA, which also covers the 10 µA maximum minimum load.
- **Dropout depends on headroom.** The 2 A row is given with 0.5 V and 0.6 V of headroom.
- **Status is not established.** The datasheet is dated 2009.
