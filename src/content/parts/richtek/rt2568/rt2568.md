## How it works

The RT2568 is a linear sink/source regulator with a high-side N-MOSFET for sourcing and a low-side N-MOSFET for sinking. An error amplifier drives them so the voltage on SENSE tracks REFIN; for DDR memory REFIN is a divider from the memory supply, so VTT is half of it.

**Two supplies.** VIN (1.1 to 3.5 V) feeds the power stage, and in the datasheet's circuit it is also where the REFIN divider starts. VCNTL (2.9 to 5.5 V) supplies all the control circuits. The datasheet's guidelines say to take the REFIN divider from VIN on a separate trace from the one that carries the load current, so the reference does not sag with trace loss under load, or to give REFIN its own source.

**Reference buffer.** REFOUT equals the REFIN voltage and can source or sink 10 mA, for the memory's VREF pin.

**Enable.** For DDR memory EN is driven by SLP_S3, which turns VTT off in suspend to RAM; the output is discharged through about 18 Ω.

**Power-good.** PGOOD is open drain and goes high-impedance after a 2 ms delay once SENSE is inside a ±20% window around its target, with 5% hysteresis; it falls after about 10 µs when SENSE leaves the window.

**Protection.** The current limit is 3 A minimum and 4.5 A typical. The text says it is reduced when the output is outside the power-good window, to one-third in one place and one half in another. Thermal shutdown trips at about 160 °C. The control logic has undervoltage lockouts on VCNTL (2.7 V typical) and REFIN (390 mV).

**Capacitors.** Two 10 µF on VIN and a 4.7 µF on VCNTL in the example, and three 10 µF on the output. Table 1 recommends 10 µF, 6.3 V, X7R, 0805 parts for the large capacitors.

## Example application

The datasheet's typical application circuit uses two 10 µF capacitors on VIN, a 10 kΩ and 10 kΩ divider with a 1 nF capacitor on REFIN, a 4.7 µF capacitor on VCNTL with a 100 kΩ pull-up for PGOOD, three 10 µF capacitors on VOUT, and a 0.1 µF capacitor on REFOUT. The datasheet gives no input or output voltages for it.

## Layout

The datasheet's guidelines: make wide, short connections between the capacitors and the pins; connect the ground plane with wide copper for heat, adding vias to cut the ground loop; connect SENSE to the positive node of the output capacitor at the output for remote sensing; and take the REFIN divider from VIN on its own trace. Its Figure 2 is a layout example. The capacitor text asks for the input capacitor within 0.5 inch of the supply pin and a 1 µF ceramic near the VIN pin.

The package is a WDFN-10L 3 × 3 mm. The KiCad symbol is ours, built from the pin table, and its Footprint field is empty: no footprint on this site has been compared with this part's package drawing. Pin 1 is REFIN.

## Things to watch

- **Which rail the divider uses.** In the datasheet circuit REFIN comes from the VIN rail, so VIN must be the memory supply, or REFIN needs its own source.
- **Output capacitance.** Three 10 µF parts add up to exactly 30 µF before derating, so the text's "larger than 30 µF" is not met by three nominal 10 µF capacitors; size for effective capacitance.
- **Heat.** 2.5 W at 25 °C is the most the package can lose (θJA 40 °C/W) and the derating curve lowers it as the ambient rises; at a steady ±2 A with a 1.5 V VIN and 0.75 V VTT the part dissipates about 1.5 W (calculated), so the rating is a peak, not a continuous load.
- **Current rating.** The text says 3 A in one place and 3.5 A in another, and the ±25 mV tolerance is for ±2 A.
- **Category.** This site has no category for termination regulators; the page is filed under "Other".
- **Pad.** Pin 11 is not connected inside the package but should be soldered to the PGND plane.

## Datasheet notes

- **Current rating.** The text calls the part both a 3 A and a 3.5 A regulator.
- **Current limit.** The over-current text says the limit falls to one-third when the output leaves the power-good window, and another sentence says one half.
- **Thermal shutdown.** The table has 15 °C of hysteresis and the operation text says release at 120 °C, which is 40 °C below the trip point.
- **Capacitor text.** It says "VLDOIN" for the input capacitor, a pin this part does not have, and gives 10 µF × 3 and "larger than 30 µF" for the output.
