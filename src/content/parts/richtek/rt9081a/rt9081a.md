## How it works

The RT9081A is an NMOS-pass LDO. The pass transistor goes between VIN and VOUT, and a separate BIAS supply powers the low-current control circuit and the gate drive, so VIN can sit very close to the output, down to 0.8 V in. In regulation the feedback voltage (0.8 V reference) is held by the error amplifier, which raises the pass current when the feedback is low.

**Dropout and bias.** VIN to VOUT is 140 mV typical at 500 mA (250 mV maximum). The bias must be at least 2.4 V and at least VOUT + 1.6 V for normal operation; the datasheet's dropout row for the bias is 1.1 V typical and 1.5 V maximum at 500 mA. Below a 0.9 V output the bias row doesn't apply, because 2.4 V is the minimum.

**Enable and discharge.** Below 0.54 V on EN turns the regulator off, and above 0.88 V turns it on. In shutdown the ground current is 1 µA maximum, and an internal 150 Ω switch pulls the output to ground. EN has a 1 µA pull-down at 5.5 V.

**Sequencing.** The part tolerates VIN, VBIAS and EN in any order without damage. For the output soft-start to work as intended, bring VBIAS up first, at VOUT + 1.6 V or more, before VIN reaches VOUT + 0.3 V and before enabling.

**Output setting.** VOUT = 0.8 V × (R1 + R2) ÷ R2, with R1 from VOUT to ADJ and R2 from ADJ to ground. The datasheet recommends smaller resistors to reduce noise injected at the ADJ pin. On the fixed parts pin 2 is the IC test pin, pulled down internally by 2 µA, and should float or go to ground.

**Capacitors.** The output wants 2.2 to 10 µF effective, so allow for derating at the output voltage. CIN of 1 µF and CBIAS of 0.1 µF or more are recommended; the input capacitor should be within 0.5 inch of the pin.

### Worked example: 1.2 V at 500 mA from 1.5 V

1. **Output.** 0.8 × (10 + 20) ÷ 20 = 1.2 V (calculated), with 40 µA in the divider.
2. **Bias.** 1.2 V + 1.6 V is 2.8 V, so the 3.3 V bias has 0.5 V of margin.
3. **Input.** 1.5 V less 1.2 V is 0.3 V against 250 mV maximum dropout at 500 mA, which is thin: 50 mV of margin at the worst case.
4. **Heat.** 0.3 V × 0.5 A = 0.15 W. On the evaluation board at 147 °C/W that is a 22 °C rise (calculated); the JEDEC 461 °C/W figure would give 69 °C.

## Layout

The datasheet's layout points: place all components on the same side and near their pins, connect the ground return of the input and output capacitors together, connect the ground plane with wide copper, and avoid vias and long traces on the capacitor connections. Add vias in the ground plane for heat. Figure 4 shows an example with the capacitors beside the part, R1 and R2 on the output side, and CBIAS beside pin 4.

## Things to watch

- **Bias first.** Bring BIAS up before VIN and EN so the soft-start works as the datasheet intends.
- **The example is tight.** 0.3 V of headroom at 500 mA is 50 mV from the dropout maximum.
- **Effective capacitance.** 2.2 to 10 µF after derating.
- **Discharge.** The 150 Ω switch will pull down a rail that is shared with another source.
- **Pin 2 differs.** IC on fixed parts, ADJ on the adjustable part.
- **Status is not established.** The datasheet is dated 2025 but makes no status statement.
- **The KiCad symbol is ours and not yet opened in KiCad.** It was generated by script and checked against the pin table, but nobody has loaded it in KiCad. The pin types and the symbol layout are our choices. The exposed pad is pin 7, as the pin table gives it. Pin 2 is passive because it is a test pin on fixed parts and ADJ on the adjustable part.
