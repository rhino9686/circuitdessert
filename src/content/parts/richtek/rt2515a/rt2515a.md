## How it works

The RT2515A is a 2 A LDO built for very low inputs. The block diagram shows a charge pump (VPUMP) that drives the pass device, an error amplifier against a 0.5 V reference, a mode-select block on the ADJ pin, thermal shutdown and a reverse-voltage shutdown. The ADJ pin decides the output: with a 0.1 V typical threshold (0.05 to 0.15 V) it switches between the internal 1.2 V divider and external feedback.

**Output setting.** VOUT = 0.5 V × (1 + R1 ÷ R2). The datasheet says the divider resistors can be large, up to 800 kΩ, because of the high-impedance sense comparator.

**Dropout and input.** At 2 A the dropout is 240 mV typical and 400 mV maximum with VIN at 1.6 V or more. From 1.4 to 1.6 V in, the maximum rises to 600 mV, so the lowest inputs lose headroom. For regulation at full load the input must be between VOUT + 0.5 V and 5.5 V, and a large bulk capacitor should keep the input from sagging below 1.4 V.

**Enable.** EN is active high. Pulling it below 0.4 V turns the regulator off and cuts the supply current to 10 µA maximum. Open means enabled; tie it to VIN if unused.

**Protection.** The current limit is 3 A typical. Above 160 °C the thermal shutdown turns the pass element off and restores it after the junction has cooled by 30 °C. The datasheet's Figure 4 shows the output going close to zero during a short circuit, which lowers the die temperature.

**Capacitors.** At least 10 µF on the input and the output, within 0.5 inch of the pins. A ceramic output capacitor of at least 10 µF with ESR above 1 mΩ is stable, and Figure 3 plots the stable ESR region against load current.

### Worked example: 1.2 V at 2 A from a 1.8 V rail

1. **Setting.** Ground ADJ for the internal 1.2 V divider, so no resistors are needed. The fixed setting is −2% to +2% (−3% to +3% over the input range).
2. **Headroom.** 1.8 V less 1.2 V is 0.6 V, above the 400 mV maximum dropout at 2 A (VIN ≥ 1.6 V).
3. **Input sag.** A 1.8 V rail that droops under the 2 A step toward 1.6 V moves the part into the 600 mV row; keep the supply stiff.
4. **Heat.** 0.6 V × 2 A is 1.2 W, so with 49 °C/W a rise of about 59 °C (calculated, on the datasheet's four-layer board).

## Things to watch

- **Low input drops headroom.** Below 1.6 V in, the dropout maximum is 600 mV at 2 A.
- **Bulk capacitance at the input.** The datasheet wants a large capacitor so the input doesn't sag below 1.4 V.
- **Output capacitor of 10 µF or more.** ESR must be above 1 mΩ with ceramics.
- **Adjustable mode divider.** Keep it light but above the leakage; up to 800 kΩ is allowed by the datasheet.

## Datasheet notes

- **Application text.** The text under "Application Information" describes a fixed output of 1 to 2 V in 0.1 V steps, an external bias supply, and an adjustable range of 1.4 to 6 V; none of that matches the rest of the datasheet, which has a single adjustable part with a 0.5 V reference, and the features list says 0.5 to 3.8 V.
- **Pin 1.** The pin table lists NC on pins 1, 4 and 5, while the pin diagram shows pin 1 as NC and pin 8 as GND.
- **Input range.** The recommended range is 1.4 to 6 V while the absolute maximum is 7 V and the electrical conditions use 1.4 to 5.5 V.
- **Shutdown current.** The features say 1 µA; the table gives 1.5 µA typical and 10 µA maximum.
