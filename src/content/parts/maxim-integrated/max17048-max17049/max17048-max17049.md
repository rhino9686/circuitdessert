## How it works

Both parts estimate relative state of charge (SOC) from voltage only. A lithium cell's open-circuit voltage (OCV) fixes its SOC, but the voltage you can measure (VCELL) also depends on load, temperature, age and what the cell did in the last half hour, so one VCELL can correspond to many SOCs (the datasheet's Figure 3 shows 3.81 V at 2%, 50% and 72%). ModelGauge simulates the cell's impedance and slow chemical dynamics from the history of VCELL to estimate OCV. Because it uses only voltage, which does not drift, it has no accumulated error, needs no sense resistor and no full charge and discharge to learn.

**One cell or two.** The MAX17048 measures the cell between its VDD and GND pins, so it is powered from the cell itself (2.5 V to 4.5 V). The MAX17049 measures between CELL and GND, up to 10 V, and its VDD must come from a regulated 2.5 V to 4.5 V supply (an LDO or PMIC), because two cells in series exceed the VDD rating. On the MAX17048 the CELL pin is not connected inside; on the MAX17049 it is the sense input.

**Battery insertion.** On power-up or reset the IC takes the maximum of 16 VCELL samples (1 ms apart) as its OCV estimate. OCV is ready 17 ms after insertion and SOC about 175 ms later (calculated: 192 ms in all). A cell that has not relaxed gives a first estimate that is off, and the error fades by itself over the following minutes. A quick-start (QSTRT rising edge, or MODE.QuickStart) repeats that first estimate and is meant only for systems whose power-up waveform is so noisy that the first estimate is bad. The datasheet advises most systems not to use it. A power-on reset (CMD = 0x5400) restores every register, including the model, which must then be reloaded.

**Hibernate and sleep.** In active mode the ADC converts every 250 ms. If |CRATE| stays below HibThr for 6 minutes the part hibernates, converting and updating SOC every 45 s, and it wakes when a sample moves by more than ActThr. Defaults (HIBRT = 0x8030) give about 26.6%/hr and 60 mV (calculated). HIBRT = 0xFFFF forces hibernate (the datasheet suggests it only when the maximum load is under C/4) and 0x0000 disables it. Sleep stops everything (0.5 µA typical) and the part cannot see the battery change in that state, so wake it before charging or discharging. Applications that can take 4 µA should use hibernate instead.

**Temperature compensation is the host's job.** The IC has no temperature sensor. The microcontroller has to measure the battery temperature and write CONFIG.RCOMP at least once a minute. Table "RCOMP against battery temperature" gives the datasheet formula evaluated at ten temperatures.

**Alerts.** Five sources can pull ALRT low and set CONFIG.ALRT: low SOC, 1% SOC change, overvoltage, undervoltage and voltage reset. STATUS says which one; clear the flag and CONFIG.ALRT to release the pin. The alert is enabled at power-up.

### Register map

The unit table above lists every register. A few details that are easy to miss:

- **Registers are 16-bit.** Write and read both bytes in one transaction, MSB first; an 8-bit write does nothing. Don't-care bits are written with the rest of the word and ignored.
- **SOC** is the high byte in percent and the low byte in 1/256 %. **VCELL** is the 16-bit word times 78.125 µV (the ADC is 12-bit in the high bits, which is where the 1.25 mV resolution comes from, calculated: 16 × 78.125 µV).
- **CONFIG:** RCOMP in the high byte; then SLEEP, ALSC (1% alert enable), ALRT and the five ATHD bits in the low byte.
- **VRESET/ID:** VRESET[7:1] in the high byte in 40 mV steps, Dis in bit 8 of the word (disables the fast reset comparator in hibernate, saving about 0.5 µA), and the read-only ID in the low byte.
- **STATUS:** RI (reset indicator), VH, VL, VR, HD, SC and EnVR. Clear RI after loading the model. Alert bits are set only when they cause an alert.
- **TABLE (0x40–0x7F)** holds the battery model. Unlock by writing 0x57 to 0x3F and 0x4A to 0x3E, relock with 0x00 to both as soon as possible (no ModelGauge update happens while unlocked). The datasheet gives no contents and tells you to contact Maxim; the power-on model suits some cells.

A hypothetical read, to show the arithmetic (ours): write 0x6C, 0x02, then repeated start and 0x6D, read two bytes. If they are 0xB5 and 0x70, VCELL = 0xB570 × 78.125 µV = 3.6288 V. Reading 0x04 and getting 0x38, 0x80 gives SOC = 56 + 128/256 = 56.5%.

### Worked example: a 500 mAh single cell

- **Gauge drain** (table "Supply drain over 30 days"): about 0.43% of the cell per month in hibernate with the comparator disabled, 0.58% with it enabled, 3.3% if the part stayed in active mode (calculated). Hibernate only applies when the battery current is low, so the host sets the real figure.
- **VRESET.** The default 3.0 V suits a system that shuts down at 3.3 V or higher. For a 3.0 V cutoff the datasheet rule puts VRESET at 2.7 V or less, which rounds down to 2.68 V (code 67, byte 0x86 with Dis = 0).
- **Supply margin.** A 4.35 V or 4.40 V cell leaves 0.10 to 0.15 V under the 4.5 V VDD limit on the MAX17048 (calculated).

- Bypass VDD with 0.1 µF to GND, close to the pins (pin table). For the MAX17049 note that Figure 15 puts the capacitor on the CELL node and not on VDD; see Things to watch.

## Things to watch

- **The 3 µA headline needs VRESET.Dis = 1.** The default (Dis = 0) leaves the reset comparator on, and the table gives 4 µA typical with no maximum. Text elsewhere says "below 5 µA".
- **RCOMP overflows below about 0 °C.** With the default constants the formula gives 351 at −20 °C and 251 at 0 °C, and above 255 below roughly −1 °C (calculated). It is an 8-bit field and the datasheet does not say how to clamp it. Clamp to 0xFF (our reading), and check the result against your cell characterisation.
- **A first power-up is "not configured".** STATUS.RI is set at power-up; load the model if you have one and clear RI. After a power-on reset the model must be reloaded.
- **The default VRESET is 3.0 V.** A cell whose cutoff is 3.0 V trips the reset when it is merely at empty, because the datasheet wants VRESET 300 mV under the empty voltage.
- **Supply limit on the MAX17048.** VDD is 2.5 V to 4.5 V, so a cell that charges above 4.5 V cannot be powered from VDD; the voltage-measurement range is 2.5 V to 5 V.
- **I²C address.** The datasheet writes 0x6C for writes and 0x6D for reads. Libraries that take a 7-bit address use 0x36 (our note, from the 0110110 in the slave-address figure).
- **Sleep and bus-low.** Holding both SDA and SCL low for over 2.5 s puts the part to sleep when EnSleep is set; a rising edge on either wakes it. If it sleeps while the battery is changing, the SOC will be wrong.
- **TDFN and WLP are numbered differently.** Check the pin table, not the pin numbers, when changing package.
- **The KiCad files are not on this site.** There is no symbol and no package page for the TDFN-8 or WLP-8 yet.
- **Related parts.** The BQ27426 ([site:parts/texas-instruments/bq27426/](site:parts/texas-instruments/bq27426/)) is a TI fuel gauge that uses a sense resistor and a different algorithm (Impedance Track). ADI lists the MAX17058/MAX17059 and others as separate parts with their own datasheets; they are not covered here.

## Datasheet notes

- **Hibernate current is stated four ways:** "3 µA" (title and features), "below 5 µA" (hibernate text), 3 typical and 5 maximum with the comparator disabled, and 4 typical with it enabled. The ADI Chinese page says 4 µA.
- **Sleep current:** "below 1 µA" in the sleep text, 0.5 µA typical and 2 µA maximum in the table.
- **SOC ready time:** 17 ms plus 175 ms after insertion in the debounce text, "approximately 1 s after POR" in the SOC register text.
- **VCELL units on the MAX17049.** Table 2 gives 78.125 µV "per cell" and does not say whether the MAX17049's VCELL word is the pack voltage (with a coarser step) or a per-cell figure. The same ambiguity applies to the 20 mV VALRT step and the 40 mV VRESET step. Check on hardware before relying on a number.
- **Figure 15 and the VDD capacitor.** The pin table says to bypass VDD with 0.1 µF, but Figure 15 draws the capacitor on the CELL node and leaves VDD, fed from the system output, without one.
- **Voltage-measurement range 2.5 to 5 V** for the MAX17048 against a VDD of 2.5 to 4.5 V.
- **Write limit.** The text says writes beyond 0x4F are ignored, yet CMD at 0xFE accepts the reset command.
- **MODE register.** Table 2 marks it write-only, while HibStat is described as read-only status.
- **Operating range −40 to +85 °C,** while the electrical characteristics are given for −20 to +70 °C.
- **Second voltage-error row** (±20 mV) has no condition printed.
- **CRATE** has no stated sign convention or number format; the plot of CRATE suggests it is signed (our reading).
- **ActThr text** reads "|OCV − CELL|" garbled in the text layer; we take it as OCV against VCELL.
- **"SDL"** for SCL in the application text.
- **Revision history:** the datasheet lists corrections to the byte order (Rev 1), the hibernate register names (Rev 2) and the VRESET recommendation (Rev 6); older copies may differ.
