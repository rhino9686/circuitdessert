## How the footprint was made

The footprint is the "Recommended PCB Layout (Reference only)" on PDF page 11 of the SY6103 datasheet (Rev. 1.0A): five pads of 1.05 × 2.85 mm on a 1.70 mm pitch, and a tab pad shaped like a T: 10.80 mm wide for the top 1.90 mm, then 8.20 mm wide, 8.12 mm high in all, 15.90 mm from the tab end to the lead ends. The tab pad is a custom-shaped pad that carries pin 3's number, because the datasheet says the tab is GND, pin 3. The SQ24333 datasheet's page 11 has the same drawing and dimensions, so it is listed as compared. The numbers were read from the drawing on a rendered page, not measured in the PDF's vector paths, so the pad positions are checked against the printed dimensions only.

Everything else is ours, not the datasheet's: the pad corner radius (25% of the pad width), the 0.25 mm courtyard (it also clears the body), the silkscreen line beside pad 1, and the body outline on the fab layer. The datasheet does not place the package on the land pattern, so the outline is the midpoint of the printed body width and the overall length, set on the tab end of the pattern, with a pin 1 chamfer.

## Orientation

Pin 1 is at the left, the five pads are at the bottom and the tab pad is at the top, which is the datasheet's land-pattern drawing as it is drawn. Seen from the top of the package, with the leads pointing down, pin 1 is on the left.

## Things to watch

- **The tab is a pad and carries heat.** It is the same net as pin 3 (GND). The datasheet's θJA of 24.5 °C/W depends on copper area, so give the tab pad as much copper as the board allows.
- **The package outline is ours.** The fab layer shows a body at the midpoint of the printed ranges, placed on the tab end of the land pattern. It shows where the package goes only roughly.
- **The land pattern is marked reference only.** Check the tab pad's size and the paste coverage with your assembly house.
- **Other makers' TO-263-5 packages are not compared.** This page has been compared with the SY6103 and SQ24333 drawings only.
