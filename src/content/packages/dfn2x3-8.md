## How the footprint was made

The outline is on PDF page 10 of the SQ24345H datasheet (Rev. 0.9) and the land pattern on page 11: eight pads of 0.30 × 0.55 mm on a 0.50 mm pitch in two rows 2.85 mm apart centre to centre, and a 1.80 × 1.70 mm rectangular exposed pad. The numbers were read from the drawing on a rendered page, not measured in the PDF's vector paths, so the pad positions are checked against the printed dimensions only.

Everything else is ours, not the datasheet's: the pad corner radius (25% of the pad width; the drawing gives none), the 0.25 mm courtyard, the silkscreen along the two sides with a tick at pin 1, the body outline on the fab layer (the midpoint of the printed size range, with a pin 1 chamfer), and the exposed pad's number, 9. The datasheet's pin table calls it a pad without a number.

## Orientation

The footprint uses KiCad's usual orientation for a two-row package: pin 1 at top left, pins 1 to 4 down the left side and pins 5 to 8 up the right side. The datasheet's land-pattern drawing shows the pad rows running across the page, with pin 1 under the dot in the outline's top view at the bottom left. The footprint is that land pattern turned 90° clockwise, which puts pin 1 at the top left.

## Things to watch

- **The exposed pad is for heat.** The datasheet says it is ground. In the symbol it is numbered 9, which is our number.
- **A chamfered corner is not drawn.** The package's exposed pad has one chamfered corner in the outline drawing as a pin 1 marker. The land pattern is a plain rectangle, and so is the footprint's pad.
- **The land pattern is marked reference only.** Adjust the pads for your own assembly process.
- **Only the SQ24345H has been compared with this drawing.** Other DFN2×3-8 packages from other makers may have other pads.
- **Not yet opened in KiCad.** The file was generated by script and checked against the datasheet's printed dimensions by an independent parse, but it has not been loaded in KiCad.
