## How the footprint was made

The pads are the datasheet's "Recommended Pad Layout" (SY26533, Rev 1.0, page 13): six pads, each 1.00 mm long across the body and 0.60 mm wide, on a 0.95 mm pitch, with the two rows 2.40 mm apart centre to centre. We read the numbers from the drawing and checked them by measuring the pad outlines in the page image, which agree with the printed values to within 1%.

Everything else is ours, not the datasheet's: the pad corner radius (25% of the shorter side), the 0.25 mm courtyard, the silkscreen over the two body ends with its extension on the pin 1 side, and the body outline on the fab layer, drawn at the midpoint of the datasheet's size ranges. The datasheet says nothing about solder mask or paste openings, so the footprint leaves them at KiCad's defaults.

## Orientation

The footprint uses KiCad's usual orientation for this package: pin 1 at top left, pins 1 to 3 down the left side and 4 to 6 up the right side. That matches the pinout figure in the datasheet. The outline drawing on page 13 is turned 90° from it, with pin 1 (the dot) at bottom left.

## Things to watch

- **One datasheet compared so far.** The footprint comes from the SY26533 drawing. The other parts listed below name the same package, but their own drawings have not been compared with this one.
- **The pad layout carries no tolerance.** The datasheet gives ranges for the body and leads but single values for the pads.
- **Similar names are not the same page.** Parts listed as TSOT23-6 or SOT23-5 elsewhere on the site are not covered by this page, and we have not compared their drawings with this one.
