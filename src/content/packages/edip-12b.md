## How the footprint was made

The pads are the "Mounting Hole Pattern Dimensions" printed under the eDIP-12B (V package) drawing in the TOP264-271 datasheet (Rev. F, PDF page 36, drawing PI-5556a): holes on a 1.78 mm pitch, two rows 10.16 mm apart, 0.76 mm drill, 1.27 mm round pad and a 1.42 mm solder-mask opening. The pattern has no hole at pin 5, so neither does the footprint. Pin 6 sits two pitches (3.56 mm) from pin 4, directly across from pin 7. The body outline on the fab layer is the drawing's 10.16 × 8.89 mm body.

The positions were read from the rendered drawing and its printed dimensions, not from the PDF's vector paths.

Everything else is ours, not the datasheet's: pin 1 is a rounded square instead of round (the usual KiCad marker; same 1.27 mm size), the 0.25 mm courtyard, the silkscreen across the two body ends with the top line running out over pin 1, and the pin 1 chamfer on the fab outline. The solder-mask opening is set per pad (a 0.075 mm margin) to give the datasheet's 1.42 mm.

A SamacSys library for the TOP264VG, which the site's author downloaded, was used as a cross-check only; nothing from it is copied here (its licence does not allow passing its models on). It differs from the datasheet in four ways: its pins are numbered the other way round (see Orientation), it has a plated hole at pin 5, its rows are 10.46 mm apart (the drawing's "Ref." lead span) instead of the hole pattern's 10.16 mm, and its pads are 1.18 mm with a 0.78 mm drill.

## Orientation

This package is numbered the other way round from an ordinary DIP. Seen from above, pin 1 is at the top right and the numbers run clockwise: pins 1 to 6 down the right side, 7 to 12 up the left side. The datasheet's pin configuration (Figure 4, "Top View"), the package drawing's top and bottom views, and the V-package layout example (Figure 29, with the SOURCE pins facing the heat-sink copper) all agree. The footprint follows the datasheet's top view as drawn, so pin 1 is at the top right, not KiCad's usual top left. The eSOP-12B (K package) has the same pin numbers but an ordinary counter-clockwise order, so the two footprints are mirror images.

## Things to watch

- **A footprint numbered like an ordinary DIP is mirrored.** Built that way, the SOURCE pins and the V, X, C, F and DRAIN pins swap sides. The SamacSys TOP264VG footprint is numbered this way.
- **Leave pin 5 out.** The datasheet's hole pattern has no hole there, which keeps DRAIN (pin 6) 2.29 mm, pad edge to pad edge, from the nearest other pad (F, pin 4). A plated hole at pin 5 would sit 0.6 mm from the DRAIN pad in a footprint like SamacSys's, at up to 725 V.
- **The exposed pad faces up.** It is tied to SOURCE inside the package. The datasheet says a heat sink on it should not be tied to any primary ground or SOURCE node on the board.
- **Only the TOP264-271 drawing was compared.** Other Power Integrations parts in an eDIP-12B would need their own drawing checked.
- **Not yet opened in KiCad.** The file was generated by script and checked against the printed dimensions by an independent parse, but it has not been loaded in KiCad.
