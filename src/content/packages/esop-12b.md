## How the footprint was made

The pads are the "Land Pattern Dimensions" printed under the eSOP-12B (K package) drawing in the TOP264-271 datasheet (Rev. F, PDF page 37, drawing PI-5748a): eleven pads 1.70 mm long across the body and 0.71 mm wide, drawn with round ends, on a 1.78 mm pitch, with the two rows 10.90 mm apart centre to centre. There is no pad at pin 5, so pin 6 sits two pitches (3.56 mm) below pin 4, across from pin 7. The exposed pad's land is a plain 5.51 × 8.15 mm rectangle in the middle; the drawing's note 7 says the pad is nominally centred. The body outline on the fab layer is the drawing's 10.16 × 8.89 mm body.

The positions were read from the rendered drawing and its printed dimensions, not from the PDF's vector paths.

Ours, not the datasheet's: the exposed pad's number, 13 (the datasheet doesn't number it), the 0.25 mm courtyard, the silkscreen across the two body ends with the top line running out over pin 1, and the pin 1 chamfer on the fab outline. The datasheet gives no solder-mask or stencil details, so mask and paste are at KiCad's defaults, which puts paste over the whole exposed pad.

## Orientation

The footprint uses KiCad's usual orientation: pin 1 at top left, pins 1 to 6 down the left side and 7 to 12 up the right side. The datasheet's land pattern is drawn the same way, and its pin configuration (Figure 4) agrees. This is the ordinary order for a surface-mount package, and it is the mirror image of the eDIP-12B (V package), which has the same pin numbers but runs clockwise seen from above.

## Things to watch

- **Use the K symbol with this footprint.** The TOP264-271 symbol file has one symbol per package; `TOP264-271_eSOP-12B` has the extra SOURCE pin numbered 13 for the exposed pad.
- **The exposed pad is SOURCE.** The datasheet says it may be soldered straight to a copper area for heat sinking, and its K-package layout example (Figure 30) runs that copper to the SOURCE pins. Keep the area on the SOURCE side of the layout, away from DRAIN.
- **Pin 5 is left out on purpose.** DRAIN (pin 6) is 2.85 mm, pad edge to pad edge, from the nearest signal pad (F, pin 4) and 1.85 mm from the exposed pad.
- **Paste over the exposed pad.** A full-size aperture on a 5.51 × 8.15 mm pad can float the part or leave voids. Many assemblers split it into a grid of smaller openings; ask yours.
- **Only the TOP264-271 drawing was compared.** Other Power Integrations parts in an eSOP-12B would need their own drawing checked.
- **Not yet opened in KiCad.** The file was generated by script and checked against the printed dimensions by an independent parse, but it has not been loaded in KiCad.
