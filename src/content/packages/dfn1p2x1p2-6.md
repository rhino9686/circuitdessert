## How the footprint was made

The footprint is the "Recommended PCB layout (Reference only)" on PDF page 11 of the SY6307B datasheet (Rev. 1.0a): six pads of 0.25 × 0.40 mm on a 0.40 mm pitch in two rows, 1.50 mm apart from outer end to outer end, which puts the rows 1.10 mm apart centre to centre (our subtraction). The exposed pad is a 1.05 × 0.40 mm rectangle. The numbers were read from the drawing on a rendered page, not measured in the PDF's vector paths, so the pad positions are checked against the printed dimensions only.

Everything else is ours, not the datasheet's: the pad corner radius (25% of the pad width; the drawing gives none), the 0.25 mm courtyard, the silkscreen along the two sides with a tick at pin 1, the body outline on the fab layer (the midpoint of the printed size range, with a pin 1 chamfer), and the exposed pad's number, 7. The datasheet's pin table lists six pins and does not mention the pad.

## Orientation

The footprint uses KiCad's usual orientation for a two-row package: pin 1 at top left, pins 1 to 3 down the left side and pins 4 to 6 up the right side. The datasheet's land-pattern drawing shows the pad rows running across the page, with pin 1 under the dot in the outline's top view at the bottom left. The footprint is that land pattern turned 90° clockwise, which puts pin 1 at the top left.

## Things to watch

- **The datasheet does not say what the exposed pad is.** The outline and land pattern show it, but the pin table and the text do not give it a function, so the symbol has no pin for it and the pad is left unconnected in a new design. Ask the maker before tying it to a net.
- **A chamfered corner is not drawn.** The package's exposed pad has one chamfered corner in the outline drawing as a pin 1 marker. The land pattern is a plain rectangle, and so is the footprint's pad.
- **The land pattern is marked reference only.** The pads are small (0.25 mm wide); adjust them for your own assembly process.
- **Only the SY6307B has been compared with this drawing.** Other DFN1.2×1.2-6 packages may have other pads.
- **Not yet opened in KiCad.** The file was generated by script and checked against the datasheet's printed dimensions by an independent parse, but it has not been loaded in KiCad.
