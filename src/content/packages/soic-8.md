## How the footprint was made

The footprint is the land pattern in the INA240-Q1 datasheet (SBOS808E, PDF page 33, "Example Board Layout" for drawing D0008A, "SOIC, 1.75 mm max height"): eight pads, each 1.55 mm long across the body and 0.60 mm wide, on a 1.27 mm pitch, with the two rows 5.40 mm apart centre to centre (the datasheet dimensions the rows centre to centre). There is no exposed pad. The package outline on page 32 gives the body and lead sizes listed above.

The numbers were read from the drawing on a rendered page. Unlike the SON-8 and VSON-14 pages, we have not measured this drawing's vector paths, so the pad positions are checked against the printed dimensions only.

The Silergy SA59131 names the same package SOP8. Its datasheet shows a pad layout marked "Reference Only" with the same pad size, pitch and row spacing (1.55 × 0.60 mm, 1.27 mm, 5.40 mm), so the SA59131 is listed as compared. Its outline table gives slightly different ranges (body 4.70–5.10 × 3.80–4.00 mm, lead span 5.80–6.20 mm, height 1.30–1.75 mm), and the outline here is TI's.

Everything else is ours, not the datasheets': the corner radius of the pads (25% of the shorter side), the 0.25 mm courtyard, the silkscreen over the two body ends with an extension on the pin 1 side, and the body outline on the fab layer at the midpoint of the size ranges, with a pin 1 chamfer. The datasheet also gives solder-mask details dimensioned at 0.07 mm and a stencil example for a 0.125 mm sheet. They are examples for the board maker to adjust, so mask and paste are left at KiCad's defaults.

The footprint is the same as the SO8E one (a different package page) with the exposed pad removed.

## Orientation

The footprint uses KiCad's usual orientation: pin 1 at top left, pins 1 to 4 down the left side and pins 5 to 8 up the right side. Both parts that use this package number their pins differently in the TSSOP-8, so use the symbol made for this package: each part's symbol file holds one symbol for each package.

## Things to watch

- **This footprint is not for the TSSOP-8.** The two packages number the pins differently on both parts that use them, and they have different pads. Use the symbol for the package you are building.
- **The SA59131's own outline ranges differ a little from TI's.** The pads agree. The body, lead span and height ranges are not identical, so check the Silergy drawing if the fit is tight.
- **Other parts that call a package SOP8 are not compared.** The SQ82535 also names SOP8, so it is listed below as "Not yet". The SY5867Z names SO8, which this page does not match.
- **Pads are an example.** The datasheet says IPC-7351 may have alternate designs. Adjust the pads for your own assembly process.
- **Not yet opened in KiCad.** The file was generated by script and checked against the printed dimensions by an independent parse, but it has not been loaded in KiCad.
