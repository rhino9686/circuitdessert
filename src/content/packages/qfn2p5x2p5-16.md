## How the footprint was made

The footprint is the "Recommended PCB Layout (Reference only)" on PDF page 18 of the SY8388B datasheet (Rev. 0.9A): sixteen pads of 0.25 × 0.80 mm on a 0.45 mm pitch, four to a side, 3.20 mm overall from outer end to outer end (so the pad centres are 1.20 mm from the middle, our subtraction), and a 0.60 mm square exposed pad. The numbers were read from the drawing on a rendered page, not measured in the PDF's vector paths, so the pad positions are checked against the printed dimensions only. In the drawing the four corner pads have a 0.15 mm 45° chamfer on the corner facing the middle; the footprint leaves it out and uses plain rounded rectangles (a simplification).

Everything else is ours, not the datasheet's: the pad corner radius, the 0.25 mm courtyard, the silkscreen corner marks and the dot at the pin 1 corner, the body outline on the fab layer (the midpoint of the printed size range, with a pin 1 chamfer), and the exposed pad's number, 17 (the pin table lists EP among the GND pins without a number).

## Orientation

The footprint uses KiCad's usual orientation for a four-side package: pin 1 at top left, pins 1 to 4 down the left side, 5 to 8 along the bottom from left to right, 9 to 12 up the right side and 13 to 16 along the top from right to left. The datasheet's top view puts the pin 1 dot at the top left, and the land pattern is drawn in the same orientation, so no turn is needed.

## Things to watch

- **The corner pad chamfers are not drawn.** The datasheet's pads are chamfered at the four corners; the footprint's are plain rectangles.
- **The exposed pad is small.** At 0.60 mm square it is mostly a ground tie. The pin table lists EP as GND, and it is numbered 17 in the symbol, which is our number.
- **The land pattern is marked reference only.** The pads are narrow (0.25 mm); adjust them for your own assembly process.
- **Only the SY8388B has been compared with this drawing.** Other QFN2.5×2.5-16 packages from other makers may have other pads.
- **Not yet opened in KiCad.** The file was generated by script and checked against the datasheet's printed dimensions by an independent parse, but it has not been loaded in KiCad.
