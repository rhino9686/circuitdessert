## Where this drawing comes from

The numbers on this page are from the Silergy SA21340IAAT datasheet (Rev. 1.0): the "SOT23-5 Package Outline Drawing" and the "Recommended PCB pad Layout (Reference only)" on PDF page 9. The site has two other SOT-23-5 pages, built from the Diodes AP2202 datasheet and the Silergy SY6340 datasheet. Manufacturers draw this package with different pads, so each drawing that was compared gets its own page and footprint. The SA21340IAAT is the only part compared with this one.

| | Diodes AP2202 | Silergy SA21340IAAT (this page) | Silergy SY6340 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Body length | 2.82–3.10 mm | 2.75–3.05 mm | 2.80–3.10 mm |
| Body width | 1.50–1.70 mm | 1.45–1.75 mm | 1.50–1.70 mm |
| Lead tip to lead tip | 2.65–3.00 mm | 2.60–3.00 mm | 2.70–3.00 mm |
| Height | 1.45 mm maximum | 1.25 mm maximum | 1.00–1.30 mm |
| Pitch | 0.95 mm | 0.95 mm | 0.95 mm |
| Pads (long × wide) | 1.00 × 0.70 mm | 1.00 × 0.60 mm | 0.80 × 0.55 mm |
| Rows, centre to centre | 2.60 mm | 2.40 mm | 2.40 mm |

The outlines agree within their ranges. The pads do not: the SA21340IAAT's are the same length as the AP2202's but 0.10 mm narrower, with rows 0.20 mm closer together, so each pad reaches 0.10 mm less beyond the lead tip and 0.10 mm less under the body.

## How the footprint was made

The pads are the SA21340IAAT datasheet's recommended pad layout: five pads, each 1.00 mm long across the body and 0.60 mm wide, on a 0.95 mm pitch, with 1.90 mm between the two pads on the two-pad row. The printed dimensions are 1.00 (pad length), 0.60 (pad width), 0.95 (pitch), 1.90 and 2.40 between the rows. The 2.40 dimension's extension lines meet the pads at their centres, so we take it as centre to centre. We read the numbers on a 130 dpi render of the page and did not measure the PDF's vector paths.

Everything else is ours, not the datasheet's: the pad corner radius (25% of the shorter side), the 0.25 mm courtyard, the silkscreen over the two body ends with its extension on the pin 1 side, and the body outline on the fab layer, drawn at the midpoint of the datasheet's size ranges. The datasheet says nothing about solder mask or paste openings, so the footprint leaves them at KiCad's defaults. The layout is marked "reference only" by the datasheet; adjust the pads for your own assembly process.

## Orientation

The footprint uses KiCad's usual orientation: pin 1 at top left, pins 1 to 3 down the left side, pin 4 at bottom right and pin 5 at top right. The position in the middle of the right side has no lead and no pad. The datasheet draws the outline and the pad layout turned 90° from this, with the three-pad row along the bottom.

## Things to watch

- **One datasheet compared.** The footprint follows the SA21340IAAT drawing. Other parts that name SOT-23-5 are not "compared" by this page: see the AP2202 and SY6340 pages for those drawings.
- **Pins 3 and 4 are no-connects on this part,** but the package has pads for them, so the footprint does too.
- **The pad layout carries no tolerance.** The datasheet gives ranges for the body and leads but single values for the pads.
- **Pin numbers differ between parts.** The package fixes the positions, not the functions: use the symbol made for the part.
- **Not yet opened in KiCad.** The file was generated by script and checked against the datasheet's printed dimensions, but it has not been loaded in KiCad.
