## Where this drawing comes from

The numbers on this page are from the Silergy SY6340 datasheet (Rev. 1.0): the outline and the "Recommended PCB layout" on PDF page 11, and the pinout on page 2. The site has a second DFN2×2-6 page, built from the SA21308A00DED datasheet. That drawing has the same 0.65 mm pitch and the same body size but different pads and a plain rectangular exposed pad, so this drawing gets its own page and footprint. The SY6340 is the only part compared with this one.

## How the footprint was made

The pads are the SY6340 datasheet's recommended PCB layout: five pads 0.50 mm long and 0.38 mm wide on a 0.65 mm pitch, and an exposed pad that is a 1.70 × 0.90 mm block with a stem running out to the position of one lead. We read the numbers from the drawing on a render at 250 to 300 dpi, not from the PDF's vector paths. The drawing is not exactly to scale, so the printed values are the ones used.

Two things are not printed and are ours. The row spacing: the drawing gives the block (0.90), the pad length (0.50) and the gap between the block and the pads on the stem's opposite side (0.15), but not the gap on the stem side. It is drawn the same, so we use 0.15 there too, which puts the rows 0.25 + 0.15 + 0.90 + 0.15 + 0.25 = 1.70 mm apart centre to centre. The stem's width: not given, drawn as wide as a pad, so 0.38 mm. Check both against your own process before using the footprint.

The exposed pad is one pad, numbered 5: it is the pad of pin 5 and the block together. Everything else is also ours: the pad corner radius (25% of the pad width), the 0.25 mm courtyard, the silkscreen along the two sides with a tick at pin 1, and the body outline on the fab layer (the midpoint of the printed size range, with a pin 1 chamfer).

## Orientation

The footprint uses KiCad's usual orientation for a two-row package: pin 1 at top left, pins 1 to 3 down the left side and pins 4 to 6 up the right side, so pin 5, with the stem, is at the middle of the right side. The datasheet's drawings do not settle that on their own: the pad layout and the bottom view show the stem at the middle of one row, but do not say which row holds pin 1 (the bottom view is not labelled with pin numbers). The pinout on page 2 does: it draws the exposed pad joined to pin 5 and labels it "GND", and the footprint follows it.

## Things to watch

- **The stem joins the pad to pin 5.** The pad is ground, the same net as pin 5, so it needs no separate connection. The datasheet's pin table lists pin 5 as GND and does not mention the pad.
- **One datasheet compared.** The footprint follows the SY6340 drawing. Other DFN2×2-6 packages from other makers may have other pads.
- **Two values are ours.** The row spacing (1.70 mm) and the stem width (0.38 mm) are not printed, see above.
- **The pad's corners are not drawn.** The package's pad outline has rounded corners in the bottom view; the land pattern is drawn with square ones, and so is the footprint.
- **Not yet opened in KiCad.** The file was generated by script and checked against the datasheet's printed dimensions, but it has not been loaded in KiCad.
