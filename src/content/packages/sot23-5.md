## Where this drawing comes from

The numbers on this page are from the Diodes AP2202 datasheet (DS38214 Rev. 6-2): the outline on page 22 and the "Suggested Pad Layout" on page 24. Manufacturers draw this package with different pads, so the Silergy SA21340IAAT and SY6340, which draw the same outline with smaller pads, have their own pages: SOT-23-5 (SA21340IAAT) and SOT-23-5 (SY6340). Each has a table comparing the three drawings.

## How the footprint was made

The pads are the AP2202 datasheet's suggested pad layout: five pads, each 1.00 mm long across the body and 0.70 mm wide, on a 0.95 mm pitch. The printed dimensions are Z = 3.60 mm overall, G = 1.60 mm between the rows and Y = 1.00 mm pad length, which put the rows 2.60 mm apart centre to centre. We read them from the drawing and checked them by measuring the pad outlines on the rendered page, which agree with the printed values to within about 2%. We did not measure the PDF's vector paths.

Everything else is ours, not the datasheet's: the pad corner radius (25% of the shorter side), the 0.25 mm courtyard, the silkscreen over the two body ends with its extension on the pin 1 side, and the body outline on the fab layer, drawn at the midpoint of the datasheet's size ranges. The datasheet says nothing about solder mask or paste openings, so the footprint leaves them at KiCad's defaults.

## Orientation

The footprint uses KiCad's usual orientation, which is also the pinout drawing in both datasheets: pin 1 at top left, pins 1 to 3 down the left side, pin 4 at bottom right and pin 5 at top right. The position in the middle of the right side has no lead and no pad. Both datasheets draw the outline turned 90° from this, with pins 1 to 3 along the bottom.

## Things to watch

- **One datasheet compared.** The footprint follows the AP2202 drawing only. The SA21340IAAT and SY6340 recommend smaller pads, and each has its own page.
- **Other parts name the same package.** Parts listed as "Not yet" below say SOT-23-5 or SOT23-5, but their own drawings have not been compared with this one. Manufacturers' drawings of this package differ in pad size and row spacing.
- **The pad layout carries no tolerance.** The datasheets give ranges for the body and leads but single values for the pads.
- **Pin numbers differ between parts.** The package fixes the positions, not the functions: use the symbol made for the part.
