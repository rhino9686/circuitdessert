## How the footprint was made

The datasheet's "DIP7 Package Outline Drawing" (SQ38576B Rev. 1.0, PDF page 16) gives the pitch (2.54 mm typical), the row spacing (7.62 reference, 7.62–9.30 overall), the body size and the lead size, but no hole or pad size and no recommended footprint. So the pad positions come from the outline and the rest is ours.

**The hole and pad sizes are ours.** The lead is 0.38–0.57 mm wide and 0.20–0.40 mm thick, so its largest diagonal is about 0.70 mm. We used a 0.90 mm drill, which leaves about 0.10 mm around that diagonal, and a 1.60 mm round pad, which leaves a 0.35 mm ring and 0.94 mm between pads in a row.

Everything else is ours too: pin 1 is a rounded square instead of round (the usual KiCad marker), the 0.25 mm courtyard, the silkscreen across the two body ends with the top line running out over pin 1, and the pin 1 chamfer on the fab outline (the body is drawn at the midpoint of the printed size range).

The positions were read from the rendered drawing and its printed dimensions, not from the PDF's vector paths.

## Orientation

The footprint uses the ordinary DIP order seen from above: pin 1 at top left, pins 1 to 4 down the left side, and on the right side pin 5 at the bottom, then no pad, then pin 6 and pin 7 above it. This is the datasheet's pinout (top view) and the top view of its package drawing, where one lead on the top row is missing. It is not the DIP-8 numbering: pins 6 and 7 are DIP-8 positions 7 and 8, and position 6 is the one that is skipped.

## Things to watch

- **Hole and pad sizes are ours.** Check them against your board maker's rules and the real lead size before ordering. A larger drill makes insertion easier but shrinks the copper ring and the gaps between pads.
- **The missing pin is between pins 5 and 6.** A DIP-8 footprint numbered 1 to 8 would put the high-voltage DRAIN pins at the wrong numbers. This footprint numbers the pads 1 to 7 as the datasheet does.
- **The row spacing is a reference value.** The drawing gives 7.62 mm as a reference between lead centre lines and 7.62–9.30 mm for the overall lead span.
- **Only the SQ38576B drawing was compared.** The SQ38576A also calls its package DIP7, but its own drawing has not been compared.
