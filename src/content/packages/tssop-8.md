## Where this drawing comes from

The numbers on this page are from the package drawing of the Texas Instruments INA240-Q1, which TI calls PW0008A, "TSSOP, 1.2 mm max height" (outline on page 35 of the SBOS808E PDF, example board layout and stencil on page 36, JEDEC MO-153). The Silergy SA59131 is pin-compatible with the INA240-Q1 in this package (the two pin tables agree pin for pin: 1 NC, 2 IN+, 3 IN−, 4 GND, 5 VS, 6 REF2, 7 REF1, 8 OUT) and its TSSOP8 has the same body, lead span, pitch and lead width as TI's. That is why the SA59131 is listed here.

It is listed as "Assumed", not compared, because its own drawing recommends different pads. Silergy shows pads 0.4 mm wide and 1.2 mm long on the same 0.65 mm pitch and 5.8 mm row spacing, where TI's are 0.45 mm wide and 1.5 mm long. This page uses TI's, which are 0.3 mm longer and 0.05 mm wider. Check Silergy's drawing against your assembly process before using the footprint for the SA59131.

## How the footprint was made

The pads are the PW0008A "Example Board Layout": eight pads, each 1.5 mm long across the body and 0.45 mm wide, on a 0.65 mm pitch, with the two rows 5.8 mm apart centre to centre. There is no exposed pad. The numbers were read from the drawing on a rendered page. Unlike the SON-8 and VSON-14 pages, we have not measured this drawing's vector paths, so the pad positions are checked against the printed dimensions only.

Everything else is ours, not the datasheet's: the corner radius of the pads (25% of the shorter side), the 0.25 mm courtyard, the silkscreen over the two body ends with an extension on the pin 1 side, and the body outline on the fab layer at the midpoint of the size ranges, with a pin 1 chamfer. The datasheet also gives solder-mask details dimensioned at 0.05 mm and a stencil example for a 0.125 mm sheet. They are examples for the board maker to adjust, so mask and paste are left at KiCad's defaults.

## Orientation

The footprint uses KiCad's usual orientation: pin 1 at top left, pins 1 to 4 down the left side and pins 5 to 8 up the right side. Both parts that use this package number their pins differently in the SOIC-8, so use the symbol made for this package: each part's symbol file holds one symbol for each package.

## Things to watch

- **The SA59131 has its own pad recommendation.** See the first section. TI's pads are larger than Silergy's, so they are probably a safe fit for the same body, but we have not checked that.
- **This footprint is not for the SOIC-8.** The two packages number the pins differently on both parts and have different pads.
- **Pads are an example.** The datasheet says IPC-7351 may have alternate designs. Adjust the pads for your own assembly process.
- **Not yet opened in KiCad.** The file was generated by script and checked against the printed dimensions by an independent parse, but it has not been loaded in KiCad.
