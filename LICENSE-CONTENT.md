- The Pantry listing and other data tables: `src/data/`

## What this does not cover

- **The code**, which is under the MIT license (`LICENSE`).
- **Manufacturers' material.** Datasheets, application notes, their figures, names and logos belong
  to their owners. The site links to and credits them and does not relicense them. Our write-ups are
  written in our own words and our drawings are redrawn, and this license applies to those, not to
  the circuit, specifications or figures the manufacturer published.
- **A design's own license.** A recipe may name a license for the design files its author shares (see
  the `license` field). That is separate from this one.

## Using the content

You may copy, share and adapt it for any purpose, including commercially, and you may keep your changes
private. You must give credit (the name "Circuit Dessert contributors", a link to the page or to the
site, a link to the license) and say whether you changed it. A suggested credit line:

The plain-English version, with notes for companies that fork the repository for internal use, is on
the site's **Licenses** page (`src/content/docs/licenses.md`).
